.. py:function:: zenith(times: xarray.DataArray, lat: xarray.DataArray, lon: xarray.DataArray, refrac_cor: bool = True) -> xarray.DataArray

   Analytical expression of solar zenith angle based on spherical
   trigonometry.

   :Parameters: * **times** (*xarray.DataArray, 1D*) -- contains timestamps to be checked in UTC
                * **lat** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains latitudes of the points to be checked
                * **lon** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains longitudes of the points to be checked
                * **refrac_cor** (*bool*) -- whether to account for atmospheric refraction. It is significant only when
                  the sun is close to horizon, in which case atmospheric refraction correction
                  is about 0.6 [degrees]. Default is True

   :returns: **zen** -- zenith in [degrees] for each time/point couple
   :rtype: xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D

   .. rubric:: References

   * J. A. Duffie and W. A. Beckman,  "Solar Engineering of Thermal
     Processes, 3rd Edition" pp. 14, J. Wiley and Sons, New York (2006)

   * J. H. Seinfeld and S. N. Pandis, "Atmospheric Chemistry and
     Physics" p. 132, J. Wiley (1998)

   * Daryl R. Myers, "Solar Radiation: Practical Modeling for
     Renewable Energy Applications", p. 5 CRC Press (2013)

   * `Wikipedia: Solar Zenith Angle
     <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_zenith_angle>`_

   * `PVCDROM: Sun's Position
     <http://www.pveducation.org/pvcdrom/2-properties-sunlight/
     suns-position>`_

   .. seealso:: :func:`azim_elev`, :func:`elevation`

.. py:function:: rise_set(times: xarray.DataArray, lat: xarray.DataArray, lon: xarray.DataArray, refrac_cor: bool = True) -> tuple[xarray.DataArray, xarray.DataArray]

   Geometric calculation of solar sunrise and sunset times

   .. warning:: The geometric calculation assumes a circular earth orbit with
       the sun as a point source at its center, and neglects the effect of
       atmospheric refraction on zenith. The error depends on location and
       time of year but is of order 10 minutes.

   :Parameters: * **times** (*xarray.DataArray, 1D*) -- contains timestamps to be checked in UTC
                * **lat** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains latitudes of the points to be checked
                * **lon** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains longitudes of the points to be checked
                * **refrac_cor** (*bool*) -- whether to account for atmospheric refraction. When true, the sunrise / sunset
                  occurs about 4 minutes earlier / later. Default is True

   :returns: * **sunrise** (*xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D*) -- sunrise times for each time/point couple in UTC
             * **sunset** (*xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D*) -- sunset times for each time/point couple in UTC

   .. rubric:: References

   * J. A. Duffie and W. A. Beckman,  "Solar Engineering of Thermal
     Processes, 3rd Edition," J. Wiley and Sons, New York (2006)

   * Frank Vignola et al., "Solar And Infrared Radiation Measurements,"
     CRC Press (2012)


.. py:function:: daytime(times: xarray.DataArray, lat: xarray.DataArray, lon: xarray.DataArray, refrac_cor: bool = True) -> xarray.DataArray

   Return a boolean xarray.DataArray specifying whether times are daytime for
   each point defined by the lat and lon arrays. Daytime is defined here as the
   center of the sun being above the non-topographic flat horizon (i.e. sun elevation > 0)

   :Parameters: * **times** (*xarray.DataArray, 1D*) -- contains timestamps to be checked in UTC
                * **lat** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains latitudes of the points to be checked
                * **lon** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains longitudes of the points to be checked
                * **refrac_cor** (*bool*) -- whether to account for atmospheric refraction. When true, the sunrise / sunset
                  occurs about 4 minutes earlier / later. Default is True

   :returns: **da_out** -- indicates for each time/point couple whether it is daytime
   :rtype: xarray.DataArray of bool

   .. seealso:: :func:`rise_set`


.. py:function:: topo_rise_set(times: xarray.DataArray, lat: xarray.DataArray, lon: xarray.DataArray, horizon: xarray.DataArray) -> xarray.Dataset

   Computes topographic sunrise and sunset based on horizon data. Sunrise / sunset
   is defined as the first / last time the sun path cross the horizon line

   :Parameters: * **times** (*xarray.DataArray, 1D*) -- contains timestamps to be checked in UTC. Must be regularly sampled and
                  cover full daytime periods
                * **lat** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains latitudes of the points to be checked
                * **lon** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains longitudes of the points to be checked
                * **horizon** (*xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D*) -- topographic horizon in [degrees] of the points to be checked, regularly
                  sampled along a dimension `azimuth`

   :returns: **ds** -- contains topographic sunrise and sunset in UTC for each point and day of
             year in input ``times``
   :rtype: xarray.Dataset 2D or 3D

   .. seealso:: :func:`azim_elev`, :func:`rise_set`, :func:`daytime`

   .. note::

      The higher is the time frequency of ``times``, the more accurate will be the
      sunrise and sunset times as it is interpolated between the two time points
      when the sun cross the topographic horizon.


.. py:function:: topo_shading_cor(times: xarray.DataArray, lat: xarray.DataArray, lon: xarray.DataArray, horizon: xarray.DataArray, sampling_freq: str = '10min') -> xarray.DataArray

   Computes topographic shading correction factor for variables

   :Parameters: * **times** (*xarray.DataArray, 1D*) -- contains timestamps for which correction factor are computed. Must have
                  constant time granularity and be in UTC
                * **lat** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains latitudes of the points to be checked
                * **lon** (*xarray.DataArray 1D or 2D*) -- contains longitudes of the points to be checked
                * **horizon** (*xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D*) -- topographic horizon in [degrees] of the points to be checked, regularly
                  sampled along a dimension `azimuth`
                * **sampling_freq** (*frequency string as accepted by pandas date functionalities*) -- time frequency to compare sun elevation with topographic horizon. The
                  higher the more precise will be the correction factors but at the cost
                  of longer computation time. Should be at least higher than the frequency
                  of the input ``times`` array

   :returns: **da_out** -- contains topographic correction factors for each timestamps in input arg
             ``times``
   :rtype: xarray.DataArray 2D or 3D

   .. seealso:: :func:`azim_elev`, :func:`topo_rise_set`, :func:`daytime`
